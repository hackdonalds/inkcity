Inkcity

Grow a teetering town of ink and watercolour. Found buildings, stack floors, keep everyone fed — and don't let the towers fall.

1 · Lookdrag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan
2 · Buildpick a building type + material in the build bar. Click ground to found, click a building to add a floor, Shift-click to remove one
3 · Bracetimber props: more stability
4 · Bridgelink two towers high up: both get steadier, people get happier
5 · Demolishpull a building down
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Sundo · save to the selected slot
Space · F · ?pause · frame the town · this help

Woodcutters, stonecutters, tile kilns and farms need 2 workers per floor. Stone floors are sturdier than timber; roofs get clay tiles when you have them. Rain helps the farms. The town autosaves every day.
