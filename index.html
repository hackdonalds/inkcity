Inkcity

Grow a teetering town of ink and watercolour. Found buildings, stack floors, keep everyone fed — and don't let the towers fall.

Gesturesone finger orbits · pinch zooms · two fingers pan · tap to pick, then confirm
Mousedrag to orbit · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan
Buildchoose a building type and material in the build bar. Tap/click ground to found it, a building to add (or remove) a floor. Hover beside a house to build flush against it.
Bracetimber props: more stability
Bridgelink two towers high up: both get steadier, people get happier
Demolishpull a building down
Keys1–5 tools · Shift-click remove floor · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+S save · Space pause · F frame · P photo · ? help

Workplaces need 2 workers per floor. Stone floors are sturdier than timber; roofs get clay tiles when you have them. Rain helps farms, storms test your towers. Landmarks unlock as the town grows. The town autosaves every day.
